Online Courses

High School Students

Academic requirement: Typically an average of 85% or higher and successful completion of high school courses meeting the requirements for university admission, including English, math, and a laboratory science.

Application documents: A letter requesting enrollment, an official high school transcript, a letter of recommendation from the high school principal or guidance counselor, and a completed application form with the fee.

Undergraduate Students

General admission: Meet the general curriculum requirements for the province you attended high school in, which includes specific high school courses in English, math, laboratory science, social science/modern or classical language, and electives.

Concurrent studies: Complete the general admission application and meet the specific requirements for concurrent studies, as outlined above for high school students.

Specific programs: Some programs have competitive entry and require a higher average and/or supplementary information. You will need to meet the specific program's admission criteria, which may include direct admission to specific majors after completing certain university-level courses.

Graduate Students

Bachelor's degree: Hold a bachelor's degree from an institution recognized by the Senate.

GPA: A minimum GPA, such as 2.75 for the Master of Management program, is required.

Work experience: Some programs require a minimum of two years of relevant work experience.

Application documents: Submit a completed application, including references, and any other required supplementary documents by the deadline.

Other Requirements for All Applicants

  • Online application: Complete and submit the online application for admission.
  • Application fee: Pay the non-refundable application fee. Some current or former students may be exempt.
  • Mobile devices: The online application may not work properly on a mobile device.
  • Language: The online application software only supports the English language.
