Graduate Applications

Admission Requirements

Applicants must supply the following documentation before the application can be processed. You may upload your supporting documents to your online application after your application is submitted (under “Documents and Supplemental Items”).

  • An application for graduate studies
  • One set of transcripts (showing the marks in each course completed since the first year of university education)
  • A TOEFL score of at least 550 on the paper-based test or 80 on the Internet-based test; a minimum overall score of 6.5 on IELTS
  • Two (2) letters of appraisal
  • Applications shall be made online

Finding a Supervisor

It is necessary to secure a supervisor for admission to thesis-based M.Eng./PhD programs. Faculty researcher profiles can be found at: Researcher Profiles.

Application Deadline

December 1 for fall intake in the following year for M.Eng. and PhD programs. Admissions are normally decided in March. All admissions made at that time are considered for financial awards. In certain circumstances, admissions are also considered for January and May intakes.

April 1 for fall (September) intake for MASc. and graduate diploma programs. August 1 for winter (January) intake in the following year for MASc. in oil and gas engineering, MASc. in environmental systems engineering and management, MASc. in sustainable infrastructure engineering, and MASc. in safety and risk engineering programs.

Fast Track Option

Students registered in academic term 7 of an undergraduate engineering degree at Memorial University are eligible to apply for admission to the M.Eng. fast track option. This option encourages students interested in pursuing graduate studies to begin their research-related activities while still registered as an undergraduate student. While enrolled in this option, a student may complete some of the M.Eng. degree requirements and potentially graduate earlier from the M.Eng. program. Students who have gone through the program agree that being able to complete a master's degree in a shorter period of time is an advantage.
