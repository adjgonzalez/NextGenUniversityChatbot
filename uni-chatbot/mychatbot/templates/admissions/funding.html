Funding & Scholarships

Financial Package for M.Eng. and PhD Programs

Memorial University offers competitive financial packages to academically eligible full-time graduate students, both Canadian and international. The value of the financial package for an eligible student is:

  • M.Eng. – minimum $18,000 per annum
  • PhD – minimum $22,000 per annum

The values of the packages are subject to change. Further details click here.

Entrance Scholarship for Canadian Students enrolled in MASc. Programs

Entrance scholarships will be awarded each year to new students who enroll in MASc. programs and are permanent residents or Canadian citizens with an undergraduate degree from a Canadian university. A maximum of two students for each MASc. program may be awarded this scholarship. This scholarship may not be awarded every year. The value of this entrance scholarship is $5,000 per student.

In-Program Scholarships for MASc. Programs

The top two ranked students in each MASc. program after the first 2 or 3 semesters will be awarded in-program scholarships. The scholarships are valued at $2,000 and $1,500 Canadian, respectively. The Scholarship Committee makes the final decision about the awards and maintains the rights of any changes to the criteria or any other issues that relate to the awards.

For information about other established scholarships, visit the Searchable Awards and Scholarship Database.
